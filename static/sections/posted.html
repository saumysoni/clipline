Scheduled

Each Short goes public at its time. You can change anything in YouTube Studio before then.

    checklistTwo quick steps per Short, in YouTube Studio

    YouTube doesn't let apps do these two, so they take a minute each. Click Open in Studio next to the Short:

    1. Link the full video: under Related video, choose your full vlog. It then shows as a tappable link under the Short. (The link in the description can't be tapped on Shorts; the @mention of your channel can.)
    2. Set the thumbnail: Partner Program channels can upload it: use Download thumbnail, then upload it in Studio. Other channels: if the YouTube app offers to edit the Short's thumbnail, pick the very first frame; Clipline put the thumbnail there.

    Clipline also set the thumbnail for search, the home page and subscriptions. If a Short says YouTube didn't take it, the channel usually isn't phone-verified yet. That takes a minute at youtube.com/verify.

    infoIf your Google project hasn't passed YouTube's API audit yet, YouTube keeps these uploads private. Open each one in YouTube Studio to set it public or scheduled.
