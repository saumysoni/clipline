Delete everything

  1. Sign in to Pit Crew and open Settings.
  2. Under Delete account, type your email address and confirm.

Pit Crew then deletes your account, every vlog, clip, thumbnail and transcript, the numbers it saved for your Reels, and your YouTube and Instagram connections, straight away, and tells Google to cancel Pit Crew's access to your YouTube account. Backups of the account database are deleted within 14 days. Clips already posted to YouTube or Instagram stay there: delete them in YouTube Studio or the Instagram app if you want them gone.

(If a vlog is being made or posted at that moment, Pit Crew asks you to wait until it's finished.)

Only disconnect Instagram or YouTube

  • In Pit Crew's Settings, choose the account and Disconnect. Its access token is deleted straight away.
  • Or remove Pit Crew from Instagram: Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites. For YouTube, use your Google security settings.

Can't sign in, or want us to do it?

Email {{SUPPORT_LINK}} from the address on your account and ask us to delete your data. We'll do it within 7 days and reply when it's done.
