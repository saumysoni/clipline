How it works

From upload to posted, in five steps

vlog.mp4 · sending in pieces

Upload your vlog

From your computer or phone, or a Google Drive link. Big files are sent in small pieces, so a dropped connection carries on where it stopped. Pit Crew writes down every word on its own server.

Get your clips

The AI reads the whole transcript and picks moments that make sense on their own, grab attention in the first seconds and have a payoff. Each becomes a 9:16 clip framed on your face, with captions and a hook that only says what's really in it.

HookTry againThumbnailCaption

Review and edit

Keep, change or drop each clip. Pick another hook, ask for a different moment, mark the start and end yourself, switch the thumbnail look, and write the Instagram caption. Every change is saved.

MTWTFSS

Post or schedule

Post now, or plan the week: Pit Crew spaces the clips out and puts them on your YouTube channel and your Instagram professional account at the times you pick. Nothing goes out unless you chose it.

Your accounts

What Pit Crew does with your YouTube and Instagram

YouTube

Upload and schedule the clips and vlogs you choose, change the title or time of ones Pit Crew posted for you, replace or remove a clip you edited, and read your channel's numbers for Analytics.

Instagram

Post the Reels you choose at the time you pick, and read your Reels' and account's numbers for Analytics. Business or Creator accounts.

Never

Pit Crew never posts anything you didn't choose, never comments or likes for you, never downloads videos from YouTube and never sells your data. Disconnect or delete everything at any time.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta. Each account can make a set number of minutes of vlog a month, shown in Settings.

What do I need?

A vlog with talking in it (Pit Crew picks moments from what's said), a YouTube channel, and for Reels an Instagram Business or Creator account. English vlogs for now.

Does Pit Crew post by itself?

Only what you chose, at the time you chose. YouTube gives Pit Crew a set number of uploads a day, shared by everyone; if they run out, your Shorts wait and go up after midnight Pacific time, at the times you picked.
