One long vlog in. Finished Shorts and Reels out.

Pit Crew finds the best moments in your vlog, turns each into a vertical clip with captions, a hook and a thumbnail, and posts it to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels when you say so. Then it shows how every clip did on both apps.

How it works

  1. Upload your vlogFrom your computer or phone, or a Google Drive link. Pit Crew writes down every word, on our own server.
  2. Get your clipsAI picks the moments that stand on their own. Each becomes a 9:16 clip with word-by-word captions, a hook and a thumbnail.
  3. Review and editKeep, change or drop each clip. Edit titles, captions and hashtags; add your own moments.
  4. Post or schedulePost to your YouTube channel and your Instagram professional account, now or at a time you pick. Nothing goes out unless you choose it.
  5. See what worksViews, likes, comments, shares and new followers for every clip, YouTube and Instagram side by side, explained in plain words.

What Pit Crew does with your YouTube and Instagram accounts

You can connect a YouTube channel and an Instagram professional account. Pit Crew only uses them for what you ask:

  • YouTube (Google sign-in): upload and schedule the clips and vlogs you choose, change the title or time of ones Pit Crew posted for you, replace or remove a clip you edited, and read your channel's numbers for the Analytics page.
  • Instagram: post the Reels you choose at the time you pick, and read your Reels' and account's numbers for the Analytics page.

Pit Crew never posts anything you didn't choose, never comments or likes for you, and never sells your data. You can disconnect either account in Settings at any time, or delete your Pit Crew account and everything in it. Read the privacy policy for the details.
