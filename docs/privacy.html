Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains what information Clipline ("we") collects when you use it, how it's used, who it's shared with, and how you can remove it. Clipline turns your vlogs into YouTube Shorts and, if you connect your channel, uploads and schedules them for you.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and, if you give it, your name. If you sign up with a password, we store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account ID; we don't receive your Google password.
  • Your videos and what Clipline makes from them. The vlogs you upload or share from Google Drive, the transcript of their speech, and the Shorts, captions and thumbnails Clipline creates, plus any titles, hooks and notes you type.
  • YouTube connection (only if you connect YouTube). A permission token from Google that lets Clipline manage videos on your channel, your channel's ID and name, and a record of the Shorts Clipline uploaded (their YouTube video IDs and scheduled times).
  • A sign-in cookie. One cookie keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use Google and YouTube data

When you connect YouTube, Clipline asks for permission to manage your YouTube videos. We use it only to:

  • upload the Shorts you approve, with the title, description and thumbnail you chose;
  • schedule them, and change the time or title of Shorts Clipline uploaded when you ask;
  • replace a Short you edited in Clipline, by uploading the new version and deleting the old one Clipline uploaded;
  • show your channel's name and the current status of the Shorts Clipline uploaded.

Clipline never changes or deletes videos it didn't upload, and never acts on your channel unless you ask it to.

Clipline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or let people read it except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires.

Clipline uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who we share information with

  • YouTube (Google), when you upload, schedule or update Shorts.
  • Our AI provider, Google Gemini or OpenAI, which reads the transcript of your vlog and a few still frames from it to choose moments, write titles and hooks, and plan thumbnails. They process this to give us the answer; we don't send them your account details or YouTube permission.
  • Google Drive, only to download a video when you give Clipline a Drive link.

We don't share your information with anyone else, except if required by law.

How long we keep it, and how to remove it

  • Disconnect YouTube in Clipline at any time. Clipline deletes its YouTube permission token and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove Clipline at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your videos, transcripts, Shorts and account stay until you ask us to delete them. Email neshrochwani121@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and everything stored with it within 30 days.
  • Shorts already on YouTube stay on your channel until you remove them there.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, sign-in cookies are signed and can't be read by web pages, and each account can only reach its own videos and YouTube connection. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Clipline isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
