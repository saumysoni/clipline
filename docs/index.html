One vlog in. A week of Shorts out.

Clipline turns one long vlog into finished vertical YouTube Shorts. It finds the best moments, cuts them to 9:16 around your face, adds word-by-word captions, a hook and a thumbnail, and, once you approve them, uploads and schedules them on your YouTube channel.

1. Add your vlog

Upload a video file or share a Google Drive link.

2. Clipline makes the Shorts

It transcribes the speech, picks the strongest moments and edits each one with captions and a thumbnail.

3. You review

Play each Short, change the title or hook, try another moment, or untick it.

4. Post on your schedule

Connect your YouTube channel and Clipline uploads the Shorts you approved, at the times you choose.

How Clipline uses your Google account

You can sign in to Clipline with your email and a password, or with Google (Clipline then receives only your name and email address).

Connecting YouTube is a separate, optional step. When you connect, Clipline asks for permission to manage your YouTube videos. It uses that permission only to upload the Shorts you approve, to show your channel's name, to change the time or title of Shorts it uploaded, and to replace a Short you edited (by uploading the new version and deleting the old one). It never touches other videos on your channel. You can disconnect at any time in Clipline or in your Google account settings.

Clipline uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Read Clipline's Privacy Policy for what Clipline stores and why.
